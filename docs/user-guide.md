# User guide

## Everyone

**Create an account.** Choose "Create an account" on the login page. Use your `@nitc.ac.in` email, your college ID and your department's short code (for example CSE). Every new account starts as a student. Faculty and staff ask an administrator to change their role.

**Find and book a room.**
1. Open **Find a room**. Choose the date, start time and end time, and optionally the room type and the number of seats you need.
2. Press **Search rooms**. Free rooms are listed first. Rooms that are taken, blocked for maintenance or out of service are listed below, with the reason.
3. Press **Book** on a free room, enter the class strength and the purpose, and confirm.
4. If someone books the room first, you are shown other free rooms for the same time. Pick one, or close the window to leave without booking.

Faculty and staff bookings are confirmed immediately. A student's booking goes to the HOD for approval and shows as **Pending** until it is decided.

**Manage your bookings.** **My bookings** has tabs for upcoming, current and previous bookings. Press **Cancel** on an upcoming booking to free the room. A booking that has already started cannot be cancelled.

**Rooms hit by maintenance.** If a room you booked is blocked for repair, the booking moves to a "needs a new room" box at the top of My bookings. Press **Choose a new room** and pick one of the free rooms.

**Notifications.** The **Notifications** page lists the messages the system has recorded for you, such as "Booking confirmed" or "Your booking is affected by maintenance". Check it after you book, and after a HOD has had time to decide.

## HOD

- **Approvals** lists booking requests from students in your department, oldest first. Press **Approve** or **Reject**, and add a reason when rejecting.
- Approving checks the room again. If someone else took the slot meanwhile, you are told so and can reject the request.
- **Reports** shows utilisation, including bookings by department.
- If a student's department is missing or differs from yours, their request will not appear for you. Ask an admin to correct it on the Users page.

## Lab in-charge

- **Rooms**: add and edit laboratories, mark a lab operational or non-operational, and keep its **Equipment** list up to date.
- **Maintenance**: block a lab for repairs and mark it available again afterwards.

## Maintenance staff

- **Maintenance**: choose a room, give the reason and the planned dates, and press **Block room**. You see how many bookings were affected. When the work is done, press **Mark room available**.
- **Rooms**: switch a room between operational and non-operational.

## Timetable coordinator and management

- The timetable coordinator uses **Find a room** to see which rooms are occupied at a given time and avoid clashes.
- Management and HODs use **Reports** to see room utilisation over a date range.

## Administrator

- **Users**: search people, change their **role** and **department**, and deactivate accounts. After a role change, ask the person to refresh the page.
- **Rooms**: add, edit, deactivate and reactivate any room.
- **Approvals**: review requests for any department, and for students whose department has no HOD.
- **Maintenance** and **Reports**: same as above.
- Set a department for every HOD. A HOD with no department sees requests from every department.
