# User guide

## Booking rules everyone should know

- Rooms can be booked **from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM**, on a single day.
- You can book **up to one month ahead**. Later dates are not offered.
- A student's request needs approval. A class representative, faculty member or any other role is confirmed straight away.
- Two people cannot hold the same room at the same time.

## Everyone

**Create an account.** Choose "Create an account" on the login page. Use your `@nitc.ac.in` email, your college ID and your department's short code (for example CSE). Every new account starts as a student. Faculty and class representatives are appointed afterwards (see the roles below).

**Find and book a room.**
1. Open **Find a room**. Choose the date, start time and end time, and optionally the room type and the number of seats you need.
2. Press **Search rooms**. Free rooms are listed first. Rooms that are taken, blocked for maintenance or out of service are listed below, with the reason. Each room shows the department it belongs to, if any.
3. Press **Book** on a free room, enter the class strength and the purpose, and confirm.
4. If someone books the room first, you are shown other free rooms for the same time. Pick one, or close the window to leave without booking.

**What happens next.** Faculty, class representatives and other non-student roles see **Confirmed** at once. A student's booking shows **Pending** until a reviewer decides. If another student's request for the same room and time is approved first, yours is **cancelled automatically** and you get a notification, so pick another room or time.

**Manage your bookings.** **My bookings** has tabs for upcoming, current and previous bookings. Press **Cancel** on an upcoming booking to free the room. A booking that has already started cannot be cancelled.

**Rooms hit by maintenance.** If a room you booked is blocked for repair, the booking moves to a "needs a new room" box at the top of My bookings. Press **Choose a new room** and pick one of the free rooms.

**Notifications.** The **Notifications** page has two tabs:
- **Campus updates** shows what is happening to rooms: booked, free again, closed for maintenance, reopened. Everyone sees the same list from the last 30 days, and it never shows who booked a room.
- **For you** lists messages about your own bookings and requests, such as approvals and automatic cancellations.

## Student

You can search, request rooms and manage your own bookings. Your requests go to a faculty member, the HOD or a lab in-charge. Fill in your department correctly at sign-up, because it decides who sees your request. Ask a faculty member or your HOD to make you a class representative if your class needs quick bookings.

## Class representative

You use the app like a student, but your bookings are **confirmed immediately** with no approval. A faculty member, your HOD or an admin appoints you, and can return you to student later.

## Faculty

- **Book** rooms. Your bookings are confirmed immediately.
- **Approvals** lists pending student requests that you may decide: those from students of your department, and those for rooms owned by your department. Press **Approve** or **Reject**, and add a reason when rejecting.
- **My department** lets you switch students of your department to **class representative** (and back).

## HOD

- **Approvals**, the same as faculty: requests from your department's students and for rooms your department owns.
- **My department** lets you switch people of your department between **student, class representative and faculty**.
- **Reports** shows utilisation, including bookings by department.
- Approving checks the room again. If someone else took the slot meanwhile, you are told so and can reject the request.

Cross-department example: an ECE student asks for a CSE lab. The ECE HOD and faculty see it because the student is theirs, and the CSE HOD, faculty and lab in-charge see it because the lab is theirs. Whoever acts first decides, and the others stop seeing it.

## Lab in-charge

- **Rooms**: add and edit laboratories of **your own department**, mark a lab operational or non-operational, and keep its **Equipment** list up to date.
- **Approvals**: you review student requests for laboratories owned by your department.
- **Maintenance**: block a lab for repairs and mark it available again afterwards.
- If you see "your account has no department", ask an admin to set it.

## Maintenance staff

- **Maintenance**: choose a room, give the reason and the planned dates, and press **Block room**. You see how many bookings were affected. When the work is done, press **Mark room available**.
- **Rooms**: switch a room between operational and non-operational.

## Timetable coordinator

Use **Find a room** to see which rooms are occupied at a given time and avoid clashes. You can also book rooms like any non-student user.

## Management

Use **Reports** to see room utilisation over a date range. You can also search and book rooms.

## Reports (admin, HOD, management)

Choose a date range and press **Update report**.
- The room chart lists rooms from the busiest. Switch between **Hours** (booked hours) and **Percent** (share of the 12 open hours per day). The axis adjusts to the data, so a few short bookings are still easy to read. Tick **Only rooms with bookings** to hide unused rooms.
- Hover over a bar to see hours, percent and the number of bookings.
- The other charts show bookings by status and by department.

## Administrator

- **Users**: search people, change their **role** and **department**, and deactivate accounts. After a role change, ask the person to refresh the page.
- **Rooms**: add, edit, deactivate and reactivate any room. Set the **owning department** of each lab so that its department can approve bookings for it.
- **Approvals**: review any request.
- **Maintenance** and **Reports**: as above.
- Set a department for every HOD, faculty member and lab in-charge. Reviewers without a department see no requests.
